Scientific Notes & Assumptions

NASA Reference Baseline: Thin PMMA sheet (30 × 5 cm, ~0.1 mm nominal), opposed flame spread; at 0.1 mm thickness, flow velocity below ~2 cm/s leads to radiative quench extinction, while flow above ~40 cm/s approaches kinetic blowoff.

Assumed Parameters (Illustrative Phenomenological Model): Thickness δ 0.05–1.0 mm; O₂ 21–35%; low-flow quench boundary diminishes near ~35% O₂ and blowoff boundary shifts higher with oxygen; critical thickness δc ≈ 0.6 mm is a qualitative hypothesis for δ > 0.4 mm; 1g natural convective drift ~4 cm/s; ~1.5 mm/s spread rate scaling at 0.1 mm.
